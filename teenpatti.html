Teen Patti

Three cards, bluff and bet. For 2 to 4 friends, with play chips only. No real money.

Connecting…

Everyone starts with 1000 chips and puts 10 in the pot each hand. You play 5 hands, and the host taps Deal to start each one.

Blind or seen. Play blind (cards face down) and bet the stake. Look at your cards and you bet double.

Best hand: trail (three of a kind), pure sequence, sequence, color, pair, high card. When two players are left, either can ask for a show.
